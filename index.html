Advisory

Market-entry risk assessment

For licensed European and global operators, and investors evaluating African markets, who want to understand the risks before spending on acquisition. Each assessment covers:

  • Licensing and regulatory position in each target market
  • Political and policy risk
  • Advertising and affiliate compliance requirements
  • Reputational risk
  • Digital visibility readiness: what it takes to be found by players in each market

You receive a written risk register for each market, a ranked recommendation on entry sequence, a compliance-aware acquisition plan, and a walkthrough call. Timelines and fees are shared on request.

Compliance monitoring

On request, a periodic review of your advertising and affiliate activity against each target market’s rules, with flags for non-compliant promotion.

Our standards. We work only with operators licensed in the markets they target. Our content follows responsible-gambling standards. This is risk and market advice, not legal advice, and we work alongside your legal counsel.
